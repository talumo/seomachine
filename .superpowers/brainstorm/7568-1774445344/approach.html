Where does personalization happen?

This is the most important architectural decision — it shapes everything else.

A

Frontend (Live Preview on Shopify)

Customer types their name on the product page and sees a real-time preview of the personalized product. When they add to cart, the name is saved. After purchase, files are generated and sent to fulfillment.

Pros

  • Increases conversion — customers see exactly what they're getting
  • Catches typos before purchase
  • Premium, modern experience

Cons

  • More complex to build (Shopify storefront + backend)
  • Requires canvas/SVG rendering in browser
B

Backend Only (Post-Order File Generation)

Customer types their name at checkout (Shopify line item property). After the order is placed, your backend reads the order + name, generates all print files, and delivers them to you or a fulfillment partner.

Pros

  • Simpler to build — no browser rendering
  • Works with existing Shopify checkout flow
  • Full control over print quality

Cons

  • No live preview for customer
  • Errors caught after purchase
C

Both — Preview + Production Files

A lightweight preview on the product page (browser-rendered) plus a high-quality backend file generator triggered after purchase. Same template, two rendering contexts.

Pros

  • Best customer experience
  • Print-perfect files for fulfillment

Cons

  • Most work to build
  • Two rendering pipelines to maintain
