# SEO Strategy Brief: Red Brick Kitchen
**Site**: https://redbrickkitchen.com
**Date**: 2026-03-16
**Author**: Red Brick Kitchen (Brandt Elliott, Certified Grillmaster – 30 years experience)

---

## 1. Site Overview

Red Brick Kitchen is a grilling and cooking content site covering:
- **Grill & smoker reviews** (pellet, charcoal, gas, infrared, electric)
- **Buying guides** (grill smoker combos, BBQ smokers)
- **Recipes** (beef brisket, prime rib, taco soup, smoked sides)
- **Kitchen tips & tricks**, spice guides, kitchen appliance reviews
- **Cookbook** (Festive Flavors: Easy Holiday Recipes)

**Core differentiator**: Site owner Brandt Elliott is a Certified Grillmaster and BBQ judge with 30 years of hands-on experience — a strong E-E-A-T signal that must be leveraged more aggressively across all content.

- BBQGuys.com, BBQGrills.com — high domain authority, commercial intent pages. Avoid direct competition on "buy X" transactional queries unless affiliate-driven.

## 7. On-Page SEO Priorities

### Immediate Technical Fixes (Do First)
1. **Add Recipe schema markup** to all recipe pages (enables rich snippets: star ratings, cooking time, calories in Google)
2. **Add Review/Product schema** to all buying guides and product reviews
3. **Add FAQ schema** to how-to and buying guide content
4. **Optimize meta titles & descriptions** across all pages — many likely generic
5. **Improve internal linking** — create clear hub pages that link to cluster articles

### Content Optimization Checklist
- Primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, 2–3 H2s, conclusion, meta title, meta description, URL slug
- 1–2% keyword density (avoid stuffing)
- 2,000–3,000 words for buying guides/roundups
- 1,500–2,500 words for how-to and recipe content
- Every 300–400 words should have a subheading
- 3–5 internal links per article
- 2–3 external authority links (cite data sources)

## 8. E-E-A-T Strategy (Critical for This Niche)

Google heavily weights Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness for "Your Money or Your Life" adjacent topics like product recommendations.

**Brandt Elliott's credentials are an underutilized asset.** Every article should include:
- Author bio prominently displayed with credentials (Certified Grillmaster, BBQ judge, 30 years experience)
- First-person experience language: "When I tested the Traeger Ironwood 885…"
- Specific test results, temperatures, cook times from real sessions
- Photos/videos of Brandt cooking (if available)
- "Last updated" dates on all evergreen content
- Sources cited for any statistics or third-party claims

**Author page** (redbrickkitchen.com/about/Brandt) should be a full credentials page with:
- Certifications, awards, competition history
- Years of experience, types of grills owned and tested
- Links from every article to this author page

## 10. Link Building Strategy

Red Brick Kitchen needs authoritative backlinks to compete. Focus on:

### Passive Link Acquisition
- **Data-driven content**: "Pellet Grill Temperature Study" or "We Tested 10 Grills at 5 Price Points — Here's What We Found" → naturally attracts links from other BBQ sites
- **Expert roundups**: Quote Brandt in industry surveys; reach out to journalists covering BBQ/grilling
- **Recipe syndication**: Submit recipes to aggregators like Yummly, Tastespotting

### Active Outreach
- **Broken link building**: Find BBQ sites with broken links, offer Red Brick Kitchen content as replacement
- **Guest posts**: Write for outdoor living, food, or home improvement blogs
- **HARO / Qwoted**: Respond as a Certified Grillmaster expert source — journalists need quotes

### Community Building
- **Reddit**: Participate authentically in r/BBQ, r/smoking, r/grilling — share genuinely helpful content
- **Facebook Groups**: BBQ/grilling communities
- **YouTube**: Even basic "how to" videos linked back to full articles generates links and traffic
